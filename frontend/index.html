Buck Converter

How these are calculated

Duty cycle
D = Vout / Vin Maximum
Ripple current
Iout × Ripple %
Required inductance
(Vin Maximum − Vout) × D / (Switching Freq × Ripple)
Peak current
Iout + Ripple ÷ 2

Everything above is sized at Vin Maximum, not Vin Minimum - that's the worst case for ripple current, so it's the point that keeps ripple at or under target across your whole Vin range.
